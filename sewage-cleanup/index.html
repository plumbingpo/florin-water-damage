Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Across Florin, Florin Restoration answers sewage emergencies 24/7, sending trained technicians who arrive with EPA-registered disinfectants and the right protective equipment so your property is decontaminated safely.

The industry classifies sewage as black water, or Category 3, which is the most dangerous and most contaminated type of water damage there is. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

No homeowner should try to clean up sewage personally. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Schedule a camera inspection of the sewer line every 2 to 3 years so trouble is found early

Signs It Is Time for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Family members falling ill, especially with gastrointestinal trouble, for no obvious reason
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • A toilet overflow containing waste that has spread onto flooring or carpet
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Slow drains throughout the house that plunging does not fix
